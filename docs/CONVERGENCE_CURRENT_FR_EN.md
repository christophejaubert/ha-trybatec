### EN — Corrected delivery acceptance criteria

- **Missing API observations are not zero readings.** Explicitly approved linear allocation of *residual consumption* between reliable same-physical-meter bounds remains permitted under #36; estimated initial zero is a labeled hypothesis, never an actual reading.
- **Never subtract indices across successive physical meters.** Calculate consumption inside each meter generation first; only non-overlapping compatible consumptions may be consolidated. Keep retired generations and unknown service dates visible.
- **No absolute supplier all-history completeness certificate is required or was ever requested.** Qualify evidence **for the selected account/lot, fluid, physical generations, dates, source and exact statistic version**. Label observed, partial, unknown and contradictory spans. User-approved unverified periods may be considered with provenance intact, but selected-scope conflicts, unapproved unknowns and overlaps must fail closed. Outside-scope gaps do not automatically invalidate an otherwise bounded operation. Users compare the Trybatec portal and report discrepancies to the supplier.
- **Versioned immutable Recorder with quarantine is an approved development path, not active permission.** Distinct IDs for generations/revisions/attempts, no ambiguous same-ID replay, verified durable readback, crashes/late writers, competing versions, rollback and Energy single-selection all require synthetic/isolated proof before qualifying a positive path.

Never turn a missing proof into success or toggle a hard disable merely to make a test pass.
- **CET kWh on the real Home Assistant Energy dashboard is post-release/post-install production acceptance, not a beta-publication prerequisite.** Before publication, validate schemas, categories/units and negative paths in code and synthetic isolated tests; do not mislabel heating as gas or invent a kWh-to-gas-volume conversion. - **Mandatory sequence:** development and synthetic tests → separately authorized merge → separately authorized beta publication → separately authorized installation → real production acceptance → possible corrections. Keep a complete testable beta objective while separating software-safety gates from environment-dependent acceptance.
